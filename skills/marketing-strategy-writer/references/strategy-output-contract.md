# Strategy output contract

Use this order:

1. Draft status
2. Founder goal and current situation
3. Strategic diagnosis
4. Strategic thesis
5. Priorities and non-priorities
6. 30-day plan
7. 60-day plan
8. 90-day plan
9. Channel roles
10. Quick wins
11. Experiments and decision rules
12. KPIs and measurement plan
13. Dependencies, risks, and assumptions
14. Access required from founder
15. Service-package placeholders
16. Diksha input fields

For every initiative specify owner, timing, dependency, leading KPI, decision
rule, and evidence source. Use `baseline required` where the actual baseline is
unknown.

# Public output contract

## Long-form diagnostic

1. Draft status and approval warning
2. Founder/company introduction
3. Core problem
4. Diagnosis
5. Evidence
6. Highest-leverage opportunities
7. Strategy hypothesis
8. Expected impact and measurement plan
9. Mosaic point of view
10. Claims requiring founder confirmation

## LinkedIn derivative

1. Hook
2. Context
3. Three to five insights
4. Actionable takeaway
5. Mosaic point of view
6. CTA
7. Approval warning

For every non-obvious claim retain a private citation pointer to the source URL or
transcript timestamp. Remove those internal pointers only after the founder has
approved the factual content.

# Growth Intelligence Report v2 output contract

Use exact H2 headings and this order:

1. Brand Snapshot — 4–6 lines, facts only
2. Founder Story — origin through breakthrough; about 20% of report
3. Growth Timeline — founder facts only
4. Business Model Breakdown
5. Growth Operating System — H3 Traffic, Conversion, Retention, Expansion; Active/Gap/Not established; no diagnosis
6. Market Context — optional and only when external research ran
7. What [Brand] Did Really Well — pure, specific credit; no caveat or critique
8. Key Challenges / Bottlenecks — diagnosis only, never founder blame
9. Opportunities They're Missing — gaps only, no recommendations
10. Strategic Observations — relevant six-lens pattern insights
11. What Marketing Mosaic Suggests — each recommendation has Observation, Evidence, Impact, Unlock
12. Biggest Growth Lever — exactly one; “If we owned this company for the next 90 days…”
13. What Other Founders Can Learn — exactly 3–5 transferable lessons
14. Expert Summary — four-cell table plus optimistic verdict ending “The ingredients are there.”

Section 11 starts on a fresh rendered page. Never add Questions Worth Investigating.
For B2B, cover natural repurchase timing and buyer-versus-end-user evidence. Every
non-obvious claim retains a private source pointer. Founder approval remains required.

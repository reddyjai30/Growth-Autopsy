# Evidence and output contract

## Evidence labels

For every material claim record:

```text
Claim:
Evidence type: observed | estimated | inferred | private-data-required
Source URL:
Observed at:
Confidence: high | medium | low
Public-safe: yes | no | needs-founder-approval
Caveat:
```

`Observed` means directly visible in the cited source. `Estimated` means a
third-party traffic/SEO estimate. `Inferred` means a reasoned hypothesis that
must be tested on the call. `Private-data-required` means the claim cannot be
resolved without analytics, ad-account, CRM, revenue, or conversion access.

## Required report order

1. Company and founder snapshot
2. Executive gist: five bullets maximum
3. Exactly 10 things they are doing well
4. Exactly 10 growth gaps
5. Exactly 5 discovery questions
6. Channel observations
7. Competitor observations
8. Evidence ledger
9. Unavailable data and private-access requirements

Every positive and gap must include `Why it matters`, `Evidence`, and
`Confidence`. Phrase gaps as testable opportunities, not accusations.
